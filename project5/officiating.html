Technology in Competition

Technology affects more than athletic training. Cameras, tracking systems, and computer analysis also help officials review decisions and give fans new ways to understand what happens during a game.

Instant Replay

Instant replay allows officials to review footage of a play before confirming or changing a decision. Multiple camera angles and slow-motion video can reveal details that are difficult to see at full speed.

Replay also involves trade-offs. Reviews can interrupt the pace of a game, and camera angles do not always provide a clear answer. Each sport establishes rules about which decisions can be reviewed.

Tracking Systems and Officiating

Hawk-Eye in Tennis

Tracking systems such as Hawk-Eye help determine where a ball travels and lands. In tennis, this technology supports line calling by evaluating the ball's position relative to the court's boundaries.

Automated Ball-Strike Technology

Baseball's Automated Ball-Strike System, or ABS, uses tracking technology to evaluate a pitch's location relative to the strike zone. In a challenge format, an eligible player can request a review of an umpire's ball or strike call.

A visual display can show the pitch's location and explain the review result to players and fans.

Artificial Intelligence in Sports

Artificial intelligence can help analyze large amounts of sports data and identify patterns. Possible uses include organizing video clips, recognizing movements, and supporting performance analysis.

The usefulness of these systems depends on the quality of their data and how their results are interpreted. Coaches, analysts, and officials still need to evaluate the information in context.

Benefits and Challenges

Potential Benefits

  • More information for reviewing close calls
  • Clearer explanations through visual displays
  • Detailed analysis of plays and player movements
  • Additional ways for fans to understand the game

Challenges to Consider

  • Delays caused by reviews
  • The cost of equipment and installation
  • Technical errors and limitations
  • Disagreements about technology's role in sports

The Future of Sports Technology

Future developments may make tracking and analysis faster and more accessible. Sports organizations will need to consider how new tools affect fairness, the pace of competition, and the experience of athletes and fans.

Technology provides additional information, but deciding how to use it remains an important part of shaping each sport.
