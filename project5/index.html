What Is Sports Technology?

Sports technology includes the tools, equipment, and systems used to improve athletic performance, support officials, and help people understand sports. From wearable fitness trackers to instant replay, technology influences how athletes train and how games are played.

How Technology Has Changed Sports

Athletes and coaches once relied mainly on direct observation and basic statistics to evaluate performance. Today, cameras, sensors, and computer software provide detailed information about movement, technique, and physical effort.

These tools help coaches make training decisions and allow athletes to examine details that may be difficult to see during competition. Technology also gives officials more information when reviewing close calls.

Understanding Athletic Movement

Biomechanics is the study of how the body moves and the forces acting on it. In sports, motion tracking and video analysis help athletes examine movements such as a golf swing, a pitching delivery, or a running stride. This information can help identify areas for improvement.
